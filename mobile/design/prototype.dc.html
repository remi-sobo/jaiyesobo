PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED OCT 2026

Privacy, in plain words.

This covers the Run It Back Picks iPhone app and jaiyesobo.com/runitback. It's run by SOBO Consulting. Short version: we collect as little as we can and we never sell it.

What we collect

·
Display name and player codeSo you show up on the board.
·
Age rangeUnder 13 or 13 and up. Not your birthday.
·
Your picks, points and reactionsThat's the game.
·
Device tokenOnly if you turn on alerts, so we can send them.
·
Apple ID linkOnly if you use Sign in with Apple, to save across devices.

What we don't collect

✗
Location, contacts or photos
✗
Real names or emails for kids under 13
✗
Payment detailsApple handles the $1.99. We never see your card.

How we use it

To run the game: show the slate, grade picks, rank the board and send the alerts you asked for. We don't sell data, rent it or use it for ads.

Who can see what

Other players see your display name, points and picks after tip-off. League members see your league board. Nobody sees picks before the game starts, including us in the app.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase and Vercel, the services that run the game. They only process it for us.

Kids under 13

We follow COPPA. Under 13 accounts are nickname only, with no email, no chat and no third party analytics. A parent can ask us to see or delete their child's data any time.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete account removes your name, picks and history right away. Backups clear within 30 days.

Questions

privacy@jaiyesobo.com. If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app first.
